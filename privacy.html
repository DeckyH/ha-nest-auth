Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This app connects one household's Google Nest devices to that household's own, self-hosted Home Assistant installation. It is used only by the household that runs it.

What data is accessed

Device information and events from the owner's Google Nest devices (such as doorbell presses, motion and camera streams), via Google's Smart Device Management API, with the owner's explicit consent.

How it is used and stored

The data is used only to run automations and display information inside the owner's own Home Assistant, which runs on the owner's own hardware. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings, or by removing the integration from Home Assistant.
